QPROOF / MISSION

Build first. Launch later.

QProof exists to explore what a cryptocurrency designed today
can look like when post-quantum security, fair mining,
decentralization, and software automation are considered
from the beginning instead of retrofitted later.

The goals are simple:

- No premine.
- No founder allocation.
- No presale.
- No hidden early-mining advantage.
- CPU-oriented proof of work.
- Post-quantum transaction security by design.
- Open participation by solo miners and independent pools.
- Decentralized mining infrastructure without privileged consensus.
- Explicit security boundaries for wallets, applications and AI agents.
- Conservative protocol development before public launch.

QProof is not being built around a launch date.

It is being built around the idea that consensus software should
earn trust through testing, criticism and repeatable evidence
before people are asked to put money or computing power behind it.

Cryptography changes.
Hardware changes.
Threats change.

The protocol should be designed expecting that.

QProof will not claim to be perfect, unbreakable or finished.

The objective is simpler:

Build a cryptocurrency that can survive change,
remain fair to participants,
and make its security assumptions explicit.

Break it in development.
Fix what breaks.
Repeat until testing gets boring.

Then launch.

QProof (QPR)
Built first. Launched later.
