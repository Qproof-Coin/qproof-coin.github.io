QPROOF / MISSION

Human judgment.
AI acceleration.
Evidence over promises.

QProof is an experiment in what happens when human judgment and
AI-assisted engineering are aimed at the same problem:

Building a fair, post-quantum cryptocurrency before anyone is
asked to trust it with money or computing power.

AI is not the mission.
AI is a tool.

Humans choose the goals, values, tradeoffs and acceptable risks.
Machines help accelerate implementation, analysis and testing.
Evidence gets the final vote.

The principles are simple:

- No premine.
- No founder allocation.
- No presale.
- No hidden early-mining advantage.
- CPU-oriented proof of work.
- Post-quantum transaction security by design.
- Open participation by solo miners and independent pools.
- Decentralized mining infrastructure without privileged consensus.
- Explicit security boundaries for wallets, applications and AI agents.
- Conservative protocol development before public launch.

No promises backed only by a roadmap.
No security claims backed only by confidence.

QProof is not being built around a launch date.

Consensus software should earn trust through testing, criticism,
adversarial review and repeatable evidence before people are asked
to place economic value behind it.

Cryptography will change.
Hardware will change.
Threats will change.

A protocol intended to survive should be designed expecting that.

QProof will not claim to be perfect, unbreakable or finished.

The objective is more useful than that:

Build a cryptocurrency that can survive change,
remain fair to participants,
keep its security assumptions explicit,
and improve without abandoning the principles it launched with.

Design it.
Build it.
Attack it.
Measure it.
Fix it.
Repeat.

Break it in development.
Learn from what breaks.
Keep going until testing gets boring.

Then launch.

Built first. Launched later.
